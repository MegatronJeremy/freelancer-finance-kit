How it works

Gross income needed = take-home pay ÷ (1 − tax rate) + expenses. Billable hours = weeks × hours per week × billable share. Required rate = gross income needed ÷ billable hours. Tax is simplified as one flat percentage of that income, so the real figure will differ.

Most freelancers find the billable share is the surprise: at 60% billable, a 40-hour week only produces 24 paid hours.
